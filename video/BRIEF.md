## Product
yesca — a wildfire observatory. Strike a match anywhere on Earth: real
terrain and tonight's real wind do the rest. GPU cellular-automaton fire
over Terrarium elevation, live Open-Meteo weather, NASA FIRMS detections
as proof the real thing happens here.

## Intent
Mini-demo paced like the first-run guide. Hook: the terrain is real and
the wind is tonight's. Beats: (1) cold open — relief mills up out of the
dark, (2) orbit — the land tilts, (3) the match — scratch, flare, (4) the
front runs downwind while instruments tick, (5) real burns — NASA's
satellites were here first, (6) end card with the URL.

## Audience
People who open Windy for fun. Fire people. Builders on X.

## Voice
No narration. On-screen text in product type: Spectral italic for lines,
IBM Plex Mono caps for labels. Silent — `music: none`.
